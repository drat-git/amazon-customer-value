## Modeling decisions made before outcomes

- Random seed 42. Use monthly scoring dates from January 2019, after one year of potential history.
- Learn a fixed top-category vocabulary from 2018 transactions only. This precedes every modeling snapshot and both validation dates. Keep an explicit OTHER category for unseen values and UNKNOWN for missing descriptions. This conservative mapping prevents future category popularity leaking into earlier features.
- Development examples have scoring dates in 2019-2020. Validation folds are January and July 2021, with strict target-end filtering before each cutoff. After model selection, refit using snapshots with complete labels before January 2022, including matured 2021 labels. Freeze the resulting models for both 2022 tests.
- Select the 90-day model first because the monotonic adjustment never changes its predictions. Evaluate every 180-day candidate after taking the maximum of its prediction and the selected 90-day prediction. Use exactly this adjustment at test and batch scoring.
- Keep identical item rows. They cannot be identified as duplicates without an order identifier. Frequency means distinct purchase days.
- Keep gift cards for the main analysis. Independently rerun the same selection/refit/evaluation protocol without titles matching the paper's case-insensitive gift card, gift code, digital code, or Amazon reload rule.
- Use customer-cluster bootstrap sampling for repeated test observations. Confidence intervals describe uncertainty conditional on fitted models and this selected retrospective cohort, not population representativeness.
